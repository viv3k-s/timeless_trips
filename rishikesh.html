Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01

Delhi to Haridwar — Arrival

🚕 Journey to the Holy City

Depart Delhi by train (Shatabdi Express, 4.5 hrs) or road. Arrive Haridwar and transfer to Rishikesh (25 km). Check in to a riverside hotel. Evening at Triveni Ghat for the mesmerizing Ganga Aarti — thousands of lamps, chanting priests and flower offerings floating on the sacred river. Overnight Rishikesh.

Day 02

Rishikesh Spiritual & Adventure Day

⛰️ Temples, Yoga & Rafting

Morning yoga session at a riverside ashram. Visit Lakshman Jhula (legendary 13th-century suspension bridge), Ram Jhula and Triveni Ghat. Guided trek to Neelkanth Mahadev Temple (8 km, optional). Afternoon 16 km white-water rafting on the Ganga — Grade 3 rapids including Roller Coaster, Golf Course and Club House. Evening meditation at Parmarth Niketan. Overnight.

Day 03

Haridwar City Sightseeing

🏛 Har ki Pauri — Footstep of God

Drive to Haridwar (25 km). Visit the sacred Har ki Pauri ghat. Mansa Devi Temple via ropeway for aerial views. Chandi Devi Temple. Browse Haridwar market for Ayurvedic herbs, rudraksha malas, saffron and herbal oils. Evening Ganga Aarti at Har ki Pauri. Return to Rishikesh. Overnight.

Day 04

Morning Activity & Departure

✈️ Homeward Bound

Optional morning yoga session or short 9 km rafting run. After breakfast transfer to Haridwar railway station or Dehradun airport. Tour ends with the peaceful sound of the Ganga and the fragrance of incense.
